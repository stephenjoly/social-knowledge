## Design Context

### Users

People reviewing deliberately saved Facebook and Instagram posts in their private Social Knowledge archive. Inbox is for finding saved material; Activity is for monitoring captures and resolving failures.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, restrained. The owner selected the light 04D pen.dev reference, requested compact spacing and borderless account controls, and rejected visually heavy progress rails and unnecessary summary cards in preview feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the existing light editorial dashboard, muted green status accent, compact sidebar, and inline table logs. Refine alignment and hierarchy without changing the application's typography family or introducing decorative cards, oversized indicators, or ornamental effects.

### Design Principles

- Capture content and current state take priority over metrics and decoration.
- Use subtle selection backgrounds and visible keyboard focus.
- Keep progress compact and legible at desktop and touch widths.
- Explain historical failures without contradicting current success.
- Preserve the approved 04D structure where the owner has not revised it.
