Note viewer: two ways to read a note in place

These mock-ups explored how to replace the old full-page note viewer and its wide black portrait-video box. B was approved and implemented in the app; A remains an alternative proposal. Both prototypes open a note over the chat or Inbox, keep your place, and show portrait media at its own shape. All content and media are synthetic.

B · Centered modal Approved

Built like Settings: a scrim, a 14px dialog, the original post on the left and the note on the right. Focused reading; ← → steps through the answer’s sources or the Inbox.

A · Side panel

Docks where Sources sits, or beside the Inbox list. The chat stays live next to it and you can resize the panel. On phones it becomes a bottom sheet.

How they differ

B · Centered modalA · Side panel
Reading widthAbout 640px of note next to a 360px portrait player. Comfortable for long notes.About 420px of note next to a 200px player. The chat shrinks to about 416px. Both columns stay readable but tight.
ContextChat or Inbox stays visible, dimmed. It is not interactive until you close (Esc, ×, or click the scrim).Chat or Inbox stays live: you can type a follow-up or click another Inbox row, and the panel swaps in place.
Portrait videoLarge and natural, fitted to the dialog height. No black side bars.Small and natural, about 200 × 355px.
Landscape videoWider left column. The dialog shrinks to fit short notes.Stacks above the tabs, so it takes a lot of the panel’s height.
PhoneFull screen, like the Settings modal on mobile.90% bottom sheet with the chat visible above it.
Build costReuses the Settings layer and the dialog focus hook. Lowest risk.Needs a new docked region, a resize handle, scroll anchoring and non-modal focus rules.

Try it (about 3 minutes)

  1. Open B from Ask. In the Sources panel, choose Open note on source 1. Press Play: the clip is silent and nothing plays until you press it.
  2. Switch to Transcript and select a line: the video jumps to that point and the current line is highlighted. Open 3 claims to verify, then Details › Show all 14.
  3. Press → to go to the next source, then Esc. Focus returns to that source’s card and the chat has not scrolled.
  4. Choose Ask about this: the note closes and the composer shows “About: …”.
  5. Use the Media buttons in the review bar to see landscape video, photos and text-only posts.
  6. Repeat in A. Type in the composer while the panel is open, drag the panel’s left edge, and click other Inbox rows.
  7. Narrow the window below 760px to see each phone layout.
